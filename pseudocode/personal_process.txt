Problem:
Check and pack the required minimum number of notebooks and pens into the bag before leaving for campus.

Input:
4 notebooks and 2 pens available on the desk, plus an empty bag.

Output:
All 4 notebooks packed and 2 pens secured inside the bag, ready for departure.

Edge Case Handled:
If fewer than 4 notebooks or 2 pens are found on the desk, prompt the user to locate missing supplies from the shelf before proceeding.
Pseudocode:
START
1. Open the bag
2. Count and Read all notebooks 
3. IF notebook < 4 THEN
	Retrieve and find the missing notebooks from the book shelf
   ELSE
	Proceed to pack for pencils
END IF
4. Place all 4 notebooks into the main compartment of the bag.
5. Count the available pens on the desk.
6. IF pen count < 2 THEN
       Retrieve an extra pen from the desk drawer.
     ELSE
       Proceed to pack pens.
     ENDIF
7. Secure 2 pens inside the pen holder pocket.
8. Zip the bag completely.
