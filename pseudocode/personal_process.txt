Problem: Brewing morning coffee using a French press.
Input: Coffee beans, grinder, hot water, French press.
Output: A freshly brewed cup of hot coffee.
Edge Case Handled: If the water is not hot enough, bring it back to a boil before pouring.
Pseudocode:
START
    IF water temperature < boiling THEN
        heat water until boiling
    END IF
    coarsely grind the coffee beans
    add ground coffee into the French press
    pour hot water over the coffee grounds
    let steep for four minutes
    press down the plunger slowly
    pour coffee into a mug
END
Why this is clear enough to follow: Every step follows a sequential timeline with explicit preconditions and clear decision checks so anyone can execute it without ambiguity.
